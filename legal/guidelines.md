# Community Guidelines

Billd is a diary for theatregoers. Reviews can be as sharp as a first-night notice, but they're about the show, not about the people reading them. These guidelines apply to everything members post: reviews, comments, lists, profiles and names.

## Not allowed

- **Hate and slurs.** Attacking or demeaning people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age. Billd automatically refuses posts that contain slurs.
- **Harassment and threats.** Bullying, threatening, or encouraging others to target a member, an artist or anyone else; posting someone's private information.
- **Sexual content** that is explicit, and anything that sexualises minors.
- **Spam and scams.** Advertising, repeated posts, misleading links, fake accounts, or selling or reselling tickets.
- **Impersonation.** Pretending to be another person, a theatre or company, or the Billd team.
- **Other people's work.** Copying reviews, articles, photos or programme text you don't have the right to post. See [Copyright](#/copyright).
- **Unmarked spoilers.** Tick "Contains spoilers" when a review gives away a twist or an ending.
- **Anything illegal.**

## If you see something

- **Report it.** Every review, comment, list and profile has a **Report** button. Reports go only to the Billd team, never to the person you report.
- **Block the member.** On their profile, choose **Block** (or tick "Also block this member" when you report). You won't see their posts any more, they can't follow you or like or comment on your reviews, and you stop following each other. They aren't notified, though they may notice they can no longer follow you or comment. You can unblock them from their profile or in [Settings](#/settings).

## What the Billd team does

The team reviews reports **within 24 hours** and removes posts that break these guidelines. Members who break them can be suspended or have their account closed; anyone who posts hate, threats or sexual content involving minors is removed straight away. If you think the team got something wrong, use **Send feedback** at the bottom of any page.

These guidelines are part of Billd's [Terms of Use](#/terms).

## Contact
Write to **support@billd.theater** about anything on Billd: a problem with your account, a post you want looked at, or a question about these guidelines.
