# Billd Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 24 October 2026 · **Who runs Billd:** Billd Media LLC, Michigan, USA · **Contact:** support@billd.theater

## What Billd collects

- **Your account** (if you join): email address, a password (stored only as a hash by our database provider), username, display name, bio and favourite shows, and when you accepted the Terms of Use.
- **Your profile photo** (optional): if you add one, it's public, like your profile. Before it leaves your device, Billd crops it to a square, makes it small (256 × 256 pixels) and saves it again, which removes the camera details and any location stored in the file. It's stored with Supabase, and you can change or remove it at any time (Settings → Edit profile); removing it deletes the file. In preview mode (no account) it's kept only in your browser.
- **If you continue with Google**: Google tells Billd your email address, your name and a link to your Google profile picture. Billd uses your name as your starting display name (you can change it) and doesn't show the picture; our database provider keeps these details with your login. Billd never sees your Google password, and if your Google email matches an existing Billd account, you're signed into that account.
- **What you post**: shows you've marked seen, liked or want to see, ratings, diary entries and reviews (with who can see each), comments, likes, lists, follows, members you've blocked, reports you send, and productions or plays you suggest (including the web page you gave and what Billd read from it).
- **Messages you send through forms**: feedback, corrections and copyright reports (your comment and, if you give it, your name; when you're logged in, your display name and username are added) are kept for the Billd team and emailed to Billd Media LLC.
- **Pages fetched for you**: when you use "Add a production", Billd's server fetches the web page you give and keeps what it read from it with your suggestion.
- **On your device**: Billd stores your theme, last choices and (before you join) your preview diary in your browser's local storage, and your login session if you join. Billd sets no advertising or tracking cookies and uses no outside analytics service (see **Visit counts** below). If you install Billd as an app, its pages and play data are cached on your device so it works offline. In the app, shows you plan and their reminders (including the optional check-in on the night and the next-morning nudge to log it) are kept and scheduled on your phone only; they are notifications from the app itself, not sent through Billd's servers. If you turn on check-in at the theatre (Settings, off unless you choose it), Billd reads your location once when you open it on the day of a show you planned, around curtain up, to see whether you're at the theatre; it never tracks you in the background, and your location stays on your device: it isn't stored or sent to Billd. Billd keeps the city you chose and the cities you looked up recently (with the times you looked them up) on your device, to put them first. To pick a city for you, Billd uses your device's time zone or, only if you tap "Use my location", your location; the location itself is used on your device to find the nearest city with shows and isn't stored or sent anywhere.
- **Your diary when you're offline**: so you can log a show without a connection, Billd keeps changes you make offline on your device until they reach Billd's server, and keeps your own profile (as you see it in Billd, including your role on the Billd team if you have one, and any account status or note the team has set on it) so it can open while offline.
  - **On the website**, a copy of your own diary, marks, lists and follows (including entries only you or your friends can see) is kept only in the tab you're using: closing the tab removes it. Browsers that reopen your tabs after a restart may bring it back with them.
  - **In the Billd app for iPhone and Android**, that copy stays on your phone so your diary opens offline. The app excludes it from phone backups.
  - **Shows you plan to see** (a date, time and theatre, and reminders in the app) are kept only on your device.
  - **Logging out** removes your diary copy, your saved login details and any changes that haven't been sent. This applies to every Billd tab or window open in that browser. Billd warns you first if some haven't been sent or are waiting for you in Settings. On the website, logging out also removes your plans. If your login simply expires, the waiting changes stay on the device and are sent when you log in again. **Deleting your account** removes all of these from the device you delete it on.
- **Visit counts**: to see which pages are used and how people find Billd, Billd counts visits anonymously in its own database. For each page you open it records the page (and which play, city, list or review), the time, and a random visit number kept in your browser tab only until you close it. For the first page of a visit it also records the site that sent you (for example "instagram" or "google", not the page), any campaign tags in the link, your device's time zone and language, whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer and its operating system, and whether you used the app. It also counts some actions (joining, logging a show, writing a review, making a plan, opening a ticket link) and records whether you were signed in, but never who you are: no name, account, email, IP address or cookie is stored with them. Only the Billd team sees the totals, and records are deleted after 25 months. Billd doesn't count visits when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
- **Server logs**: our hosting providers (GitHub Pages, Supabase) keep standard request logs, including IP addresses, for security and operations, under their own retention rules.

## Who processes it

- **Supabase** (United States: US East) hosts the database, profile photos and logins, and runs Billd's server functions (the "Add a production" page reader and account deletion).
- **Brevo** sends sign-up and password emails.
- **Google** confirms who you are when you choose "Continue with Google".
- **Web3Forms** delivers form messages to Billd Media LLC's email.
- **GitHub Pages** hosts the website.

## Other sites your browser contacts

Viewing Billd loads fonts from **Google Fonts**, pictures from **Wikimedia Commons**, and show artwork from **Ticketmaster**. Those services receive your IP address and browser details as part of the request, under their own privacy policies. Ticket links take you to Ticketmaster, TodayTix, Broadway Direct or a theatre, whose own policies apply once you're there.

## Who can see what

Your profile (with your photo, if you add one), public diary entries, reviews, lists, follows and likes are public. Entries marked "Friends" are visible only to the members you follow; "Only me" only to you. Members you block can still see your public posts, but can't follow you or like or comment on them, and you no longer see theirs. Reports, blocks and suggestions are seen only by you and, for reports and suggestions, the Billd team.

## Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete your posts, download your data (Settings → Download my data), and delete your account and everything you posted yourself (Settings → Delete my account; see [Deleting your account](#/delete-account)). If you can't log in, ask us at support@billd.theater. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA) you may have further rights, such as access, correction and objection; write to us to use them. If you're in the EU or UK, our lawful bases are contract (running your account) and legitimate interests (moderation, security and anonymous visit counts).

## Keeping and protecting data

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account removes it at once, except reports you sent (kept for the team without your name) and our provider's backups, which are overwritten within 30 days. Access to the database is restricted to Billd Media LLC and protected by row-level security.

## Children

Billd is not for children under 13. If you're under the age at which your country lets you agree to an online service on your own (16 in some countries), ask a parent or guardian first.
