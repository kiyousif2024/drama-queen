Billd support

Billd is a diary for theatregoers, on the web at billd.theater and in the Billd Diary app for iPhone and Android. It's run by Billd Media LLC in Michigan.

Contact us

Write to support@billd.theater about anything: a problem with your account or the app, a post you want the team to look at, a mistake in a show's details, or an idea. We read every message.

Common questions

How do I join or log in?

Tap Join or Log in and use your email address and a password, or Continue with Google or Sign in with Apple where they're offered. If you forget your password, choose Forgot password? on the login form and Billd emails you a link to set a new one. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then write to us.

How do I log a show?

Open the show and tap Log or review. Pick the date you saw it, add a rating out of five stars (halves allowed) and, if you like, a review. You can choose who sees each entry: everyone, your friends or only you.

Can I use Billd without a connection?

Yes. Shows you log or mark while offline are saved on your device and sent to Billd when you're back online. A bar shows how many changes are still waiting.

A show's details are wrong or missing

Open the show and use Suggest a correction at the bottom of its details, or write to us. Members can also suggest a missing production with Add a production; the team checks every suggestion before it appears.

Someone posted something they shouldn't have

Use Report on the review, comment, list or profile, and choose a reason. Reports go to the Billd team. You can also Block a member from their profile so you no longer see their posts and they can't follow you or comment on yours. The Community Guidelines say what's allowed.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Settings → Download my data saves everything you've logged, rated, reviewed and listed as a file.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete my account, then type your username to confirm. Your account and everything you posted are removed, and it can't be undone. Deleting your account explains exactly what's removed. If you can't log in, write to us from the email address on your account.

What does Billd do with my information?

The Privacy Policy explains what Billd keeps and why. In short: no ads, no tracking, no outside analytics, and your location never leaves your device.
